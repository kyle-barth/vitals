Vitals: Specs tab, four directions

A third tab beside Vitals and Apps that lays out this PC's parts, with one click to copy them as text. Every frame is live: drag a bottom-right corner to resize it, scroll with the wheel when it overflows, and click the copy control. All content is real, read from this PC (i7-4790K, RTX 2070 SUPER, Maximus VII Impact). Everything is drawn on canvas with primitives that map one-to-one to Direct2D: rounded rectangles, lines, circles and Segoe UI text without wrapping.

What Copy puts on the clipboard

The same text in every direction. Plain lines that paste cleanly into Discord, Reddit or a forum post.
